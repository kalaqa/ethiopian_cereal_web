About Ethiopian Cereals

Cereals are an important part of food and agriculture in Ethiopia. They are used to prepare many traditional meals and provide energy for families.

Some popular cereals in Ethiopia include teff, maize, wheat, barley and sorghum.

Popular Ethiopian Cereals

  • Teff: Used to prepare injera.
  • Maize: Eaten boiled, roasted or ground.
  • Wheat: Used to make bread and pasta.
  • Barley: Used in porridge and other foods.
  • Sorghum: Used to prepare traditional foods.
