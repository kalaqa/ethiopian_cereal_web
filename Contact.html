Discover the Beauty of Lake Tana in Ethiopia

This week, students learned about Lake Tana, the largest lake in Ethiopia. It is located in the Amhara region. "It is one of the most beautiful places in Ethiopia," said one student. The Blue Nile River begins its journey from Lake Tana.

Important: Lake Tana is famous for its natural beauty, historical islands, and ancient monasteries. The old information about its location is Southern Ethiopia Northwestern Ethiopia.
