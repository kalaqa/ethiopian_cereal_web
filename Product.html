Types of Ethiopian Cereals

  1. Teff: A small grain commonly used to prepare injera.
  2. Maize: Also called corn. It can be boiled, roasted or ground.
  3. Wheat: Used to prepare bread, pasta and other foods.
  4. Barley: Used for porridge and traditional foods.
  5. Sorghum: Used in different traditional Ethiopian foods.
